✦ HYPERSPELL GRIMOIRE

The entire spell system, explained — generated live from the game's own spellbook, so it is always true.

Casting & the two slots

Every wizard carries two spell slots, each bound to its own cast button (keyboard 1: E/Q or left/right mouse · keyboard 2: Enter/R-Shift · gamepad face buttons). Aim with the mouse or stick; without one, spells fire the way you're facing.

  • Cooldowns. Each spell has its own, but nothing casts faster than the global floor of — even spammy bolts are deliberate, aimed shots.
  • ✦ HYPERSPELL proc. Every cast has a small chance to go off empowered at 2.2×. The chance scales with the spell's cooldown (~1.2% per second of cooldown, capped at 6%) — slow, heavy spells roll for it; spam doesn't farm it.
  • 🛡 BLOCK (parry). A third button (S / ↓ / middle mouse / gamepad LB) throws up a shield for a quarter second: you take no damage and projectiles bounce back at the sender. Then it needs ~1.4s to recharge — time it, don't turtle.
  • Health. Wizards carry 150 HP. Below half, your hat gets knocked off (the shame). Below a sixth, you smolder.

Tomes & rarity

Spell tomes rain from the sky. Grab one and it fills an empty slot — or replaces your oldest spell if both hands are full. Drops are weighted by rarity tier:

Rare and legendary grabs get a fanfare. The same tome never drops twice in a row. Hybrid-tier spells never drop at all — they exist only through fusion (below).

🎩 The Mega Hat

Occasionally a golden hat falls instead (~12% of drops, one at a time). Claim it for a full heal, double size, and 3 casts empowered at 1.7×. Lose half your health and your regular hat gets knocked off — the ultimate shame.

⚡ Fusion — combining spells

Spells belong to schools. Hold two spells whose schools pair up in a recipe and they auto-fuse the moment you pick up the second tome, collapsing into a single bespoke hybrid spell you can't get any other way. Two spells of the same school fuse into that school's amplified form.

Fusion charges. Hybrids are not limitless — they carry 1–3 charges scaled to their power (the heaviest ultimates get one shot; light hybrids get three), and each cast is boosted beyond normal: ×1.5 on 1-charge monsters, ×1.35 on 2-charge, ×1.2 on 3-charge. Spend the last charge and the fusion is gone — back to the tomes. Your HUD shows the ×N remaining. And a charged fusion is protected: tome grabs route to your other hand and can never accidentally overwrite it.

◇ The Fusion Catalyst

A rare prism drop (~10% of tome drops) that acts as a wild ingredient: if your two held spells already match a recipe it fuses them on the spot; otherwise it fuses with your newest spell into its school's amplified hybrid. No school, no fusion — it fizzles.

Elemental synergies

  • ❄ SHATTER. A solid hit (8+ damage) on a frozen wizard cracks the ice: +60% bonus damage, the freeze breaks — and thawing leaves them Wet for 4.5s. Standing on ice also keeps you Wet.
  • ⚡ CONDUCT. Lightning-family beams hitting a Wet wizard deal 1.6× and arc to the nearest other wizard within reach for 45% damage. Freeze → shatter → zap is the classic three-move combo.
